## What’s a Consultant?

Curious problem solvers, consultants confidently bridge the gap between a customer’s challenges and their goals. A consultant:

1. Listens to their customer’s business aspirations.  
    
2. Figures out what tools (in our case, Salesforce solutions) the customer needs to succeed.  
    
3. Shares their recommendations in easy-to-understand terms.  
    
4. Works with their customer to create a plan that maps to results.  
    

Let’s take a closer look at the skill sets of a consultant.

## Common Consultant Activities

What does a consultant do day-to-day? Here are the main activities they work on through their customer’s journey:

1. **Customer-centric discovery:** A business consultant does customer-centric discovery to uncover the customer’s current experience, future needs, and biggest challenges. Then, they co-create successful strategies to develop solutions, with a focus on building ongoing relationships based on trust.  
    
2. **Business case creation:** To create a business case, a consultant breaks down the customer’s challenges and goals, as well as the expenses and tasks associated with the project. Once the customer has a clear picture of the overall project, they can approve and fund it.  
    
3. **Project management:** During project management, a consultant identifies the milestones, tasks, work items, and deliverables for the customer.  
    
4. **Implementation:** During implementation, a consultant successfully delivers the tools the customer needs to reach their goals. A consultant also provides training and adoption services for the new tool sets.  
    
5. **Evaluation:** After the implementation, a consultant tracks and reports on the customer’s progress toward their goals


## Find the Best Consultant Role for You

The [Salesforce ecosystem](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/the-salesforce-ecosystem-quick-look?trail_id=build-your-career-with-salesforce-skills) is vast and diverse, so consultant roles at Salesforce come in many forms. Here are the four most common Salesforce consultant types. 

|Salesforce Consultant Type|What They Do|
|---|---|
|[Business analyst](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/en/content/learn/modules/salesforce-business-analyst-quick-look)|- Communicates between information technology (IT) and business stakeholders  <br>    <br>- Figures out the requirements of a customer’s business challenge  <br>    <br>- Produces data-driven solutions|
|[Functional consultant](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/en/content/learn/modules/cc-functional-consulting)|- Often acts as the project lead in a consultation  <br>    <br>- Works with the customer to find transformational solutions using their knowledge of how businesses in various industries operate  <br>    <br>- Measures and reports on a business’s overall health|
|[Technical consultant](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/en/content/learn/modules/technical-project-manager-responsibilities?trail_id=get-started-with-technical-project-management)|- Develops a deep understanding of the full capabilities of one or more cloud solutions  <br>    <br>- Implements specialized, back-end changes to a customer’s Salesforce setups|
