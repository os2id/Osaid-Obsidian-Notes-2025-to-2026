Find the exact path in the terminal

\['find ~ -name &lt;,File Name&gt; 2>/dev/null'\]

For Example:  
\['find ~ -name rockyou.txt 2>/dev/null'\]  
![e5b3bcf40185658985e2ee1f2b5c3c11.png](:/b07025359dc64789b3dd79235f11ef11)
