- **Rest:** 1 minute between all sets.
- **Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes before every session.
- **Daily Walk:** 30–45 minutes maximum.   

### Day 1 (Full Body Workout)

1. **Push-ups:** 3 sets × 10 reps
2. **Squats:** 3 sets × 15 reps
3. **Plank:** 3 sets × 20 seconds
4. **High Knees** _(Swapped from Jumping Jacks)_: 3 sets × 30 seconds
5. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max

 Day 2 (Pull Workout)
1. **High-bar Australian Row:** 3 sets × 10 reps
2. **Negative Pull-ups:** 3 sets × 5 reps
3. **Underhand Inverted Row / Chin-up Row** _(Swapped standard Inverted Row)_: 3 sets × 12 reps (decrease 2 reps each set)
4. **Dead Hang:** 3 sets × 10 seconds (decrease 2 seconds each set)
5. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max



 
 Day 3 (Core Workout)
1. **Shoulder Taps:** 3 sets × 5 reps per hand
2. **Sit-ups:** 3 sets × 15 reps
3. **Dead Bug** _(Swapped from Leg Raises)_: 3 sets × 10 reps total
4. **Side Plank:** 3 sets × 15 seconds per side
5. **Plank:** 2 sets × 20 seconds
6. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max



### Day 4 (Push Workout)
1. **Push-ups:** 3 sets × 10 reps
2. **Incline Pike Push-ups:** 3 sets × 8 reps
3. **Bench Dips:** 3 sets × 10 reps
4. **Decline Push-ups (Feet Elevated)** _(Swapped from Knee Diamond Push-ups)_: 3 sets × 8 reps
5. **Wall Handstand Hold:** 3 sets × 20 seconds
6. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max

### Day 5 (Legs Workout)

1. **Bodyweight Squats:** 3 sets × 15 reps
2. **Forward Lunges** _(Swapped from Reverse Lunges)_: 3 sets × 10 reps per leg
3. **Wall Sit:** 3 sets × 20 seconds
4. **Glute Bridges:** 3 sets × 12 reps
5. **Standing Calf Raises:** 3 sets × 15 reps
6. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max




### Day 6 (Mobility & Skills)

1. **Wall Handstand Hold:** 3 sets × 20 seconds
2. **Dead Hang:** 3 sets × 15 seconds
3. **Scapular Pull-ups:** 3 sets × 8 reps
4. **Skin the Cat**: 3 sets × 15-second hold
5. **Walk:** 30–45 minutes max

 **Active German Hang Stretch** _(Swapped from Skin the Cat)_: 3 sets × 15-second hold



### Day 7 (Full Rest Day)
