==Skills I want to master as an professional:==
1- Handstand hold - Handstand push up - Handstand to L sit - L sit to handstand (in all sections)  
  
2- Front Lever (in the pull section)  
  
3- Human flag - Human flag pull up (In the full body section & core)  
  
4- Planche - Handstand to Planche [negative] - Planche to Handstand [positive] (in the push section)  
  
5- back lever (in the core section)  



Day 1 (Pull day) **REST 1 MINUTE BETWEEN EACH SET**:
0- Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes

1- Front lever 10 minutes ( Negative front lever going from up to down in a straight motion, then we can hold tuck front lever if we get tired )

2- Handstand for 15 minutes

3- 2 Muscle up then 10 push up over the bar (Set 1), 2 Muscle up then 8 push up over the bar (Set 2), 1 Muscle up then 15 push up over the bar (Set 3), 1 Muscle up then 15 push up over the bar (Set 4)

4- Pull up 10 then 8 chin up (set 1), 8 Pull up then 5 chin up (set 2 we can do negatives if we got tired but we should hold in negatives), 10 chin up then 8 Pull ups (set 3 we can do negatives if we got tired), 8 chin up then 5 Pull ups (set 4 we can do negatives if we got tired)

5- 2 core sets (5 second hold L dead hang on the bar - 5 leg raises on the bar - 30 repetition of sit up on the wall the leg should be - 20 right to left the leg should be in the wall - 20 second plank)

6- Cardio for 15 minutes




Day 2 (Legs & cardio day) **REST 30 SECONDS BETWEEN EACH SET**:

1- Handstand for 15 minutes

2- 12 Negative Pistol Squats (Doing it on a chair or a low box, if you cannot then do it in a higher chair or box, and we should decrease 2 repetitions first two sets then last set will decrease 3) For 4 Sets

4- 35 Nordic/Bridge in a box for 4 sets (Decrease 5 repetitions every set, but last 2 we do 25 repetition no less than 25)

5- 25 Jump Squats (we should decrease 5 repetitions every set) for 4 sets 

6- 25 Seconds Wall sit for 4 sets


7- 2 core sets (5 second hold L dead hang on the bar - 5 leg raises on the bar - 30 repetition of sit up on the wall the leg should be - 20 right to left the leg should be in the wall - 20 second plank)

8- Cardio for 15 minutes
